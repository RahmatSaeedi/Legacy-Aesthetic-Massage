A full-body exfoliation — often called a body scrub — is one of the most underrated treatments for men, especially after a long, dry Edmonton winter. If you've never had one, here's exactly what happens and why your skin will thank you.

## What is a body scrub, really?

A [full-body exfoliation massage](/services/full-body-exfoliation/) uses a mineral-rich scrub, worked over the skin with massage technique, to lift away the dull, dead surface cells that build up over time. Underneath is the smoother, brighter, healthier skin that was there all along.

It also stimulates circulation and encourages collagen production, which keeps skin looking firmer and more youthful.

## The four simple steps

1. **Relax.** You settle onto a warm table with your comfort and privacy assured throughout.
2. **Exfoliate.** Your aesthetician massages the scrub across the body, sloughing away dead skin.
3. **Rinse.** The exfoliant is gently rinsed away to reveal fresh skin.
4. **Moisturize.** A nourishing lotion or oil is massaged in, leaving you silky-smooth and hydrated.

## Who is it for?

Honestly, most men. It's ideal if you have:

- Dry, flaky, or rough skin (hello, winter)
- Frequent ingrown hairs or rough patches
- A big event coming up and you want to look and feel your best
- Skin that just feels dull and could use a reset

## How to get the best results

Skip heavy lotions on the day of your appointment, drink plenty of water afterward, and consider rebooking every four to six weeks to keep skin smooth without over-exfoliating.
