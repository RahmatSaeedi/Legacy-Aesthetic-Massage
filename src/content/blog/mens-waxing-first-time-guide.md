More men are switching from shaving to waxing for the chest, back, and shoulders — and once they do, most don't go back. Smoother results, slower regrowth, and no daily stubble. If you're considering your first [male waxing](/services/male-waxing/) appointment, here's what to know.

## Why men choose waxing over shaving

- **It lasts.** Shaving lasts a day or two; waxing lasts weeks.
- **Regrowth gets finer.** Over time, waxed hair grows back softer and sparser.
- **Fewer ingrowns.** Done properly, waxing causes fewer ingrown hairs than a razor.
- **No stubble.** You skip that itchy in-between phase entirely.

## How to prep

A little prep makes a big difference:

- **Let it grow.** Hair should be about a quarter-inch long — roughly two to three weeks of growth — so the wax can grip properly. Don't shave right before.
- **Exfoliate a day before.** Gently exfoliating the area the day before helps free trapped hairs.
- **Skip the lotion.** Come with clean, product-free skin on the day.

## Does it hurt?

There's a brief moment of sensation as each strip is removed — honest answer. But a skilled technician works quickly and efficiently, and it genuinely gets easier with repeat visits as hair grows back finer. Most men are surprised it's far more manageable than they expected.

## After your wax

Avoid hot showers, heavy sweating, and friction for 24 hours, and keep the skin moisturized. Exfoliating gently a few days later helps prevent ingrown hairs.
