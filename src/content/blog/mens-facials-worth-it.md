Let's clear something up: facials aren't just pampering. For men, a professional facial solves real, specific problems — and the results are visible. Here's the honest breakdown.

## Men's skin is different

Men's skin is thicker and oilier than women's, and it takes a daily beating from shaving. That combination means more clogged pores, more irritation, and more ingrown hairs. An [anti-aging facial](/services/anti-aging-facial/) is tailored to exactly that: it deep-cleans congested pores, calms shaving irritation, and rehydrates skin that weather and routine have left dull.

## What a facial actually does

A good facial works in layers:

- **Cleansing** removes the grime and oil that ordinary washing misses.
- **Exfoliation** clears away dead skin that dulls your complexion and traps oil.
- **Extraction & treatment** decongest pores and target your specific concerns.
- **Hydration** restores moisture and a healthy, rested look.

## When it's genuinely worth it

A facial is worth booking if you deal with any of these:

- Dull, tired-looking skin
- Frequent breakouts or congested pores — our [acne therapy](/services/acne-therapy/) goes further here
- Razor burn and irritation from shaving
- Early fine lines you'd like to soften

## How often?

Because your skin renews itself roughly every four to six weeks, that's also the sweet spot for facials. One good facial makes a difference; a regular rhythm keeps it.
