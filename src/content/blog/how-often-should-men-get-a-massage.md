It's the question we hear most: how often should I actually get a massage? The honest answer is "it depends on your goals" — but here's a practical guide to help you decide.

## If you're managing pain or chronic tension

Dealing with a tight lower back, stiff neck, or a specific injury? Aim for **once a week or every other week** at first. Regular [deep-tissue massage](/services/deep-tissue-massage/) in the early weeks makes real progress, and you can space sessions out as things improve.

## If you train hard

Active men and weekend athletes do well with a session **every two to four weeks**. It supports recovery, keeps muscles supple, and helps prevent the small niggles that turn into bigger problems.

## If it's about stress and maintenance

If your main goal is to unwind, sleep better, and keep everyday tension in check, **once a month** is the sweet spot. A monthly [relaxation and skin-moisturizing massage](/services/skin-moisturizing-massage/) is simple, sustainable self-care — and your skin gets the bonus of deep hydration.

## If you just need a reset

There's nothing wrong with booking **as needed** — before a big event, after a stressful stretch, or when your body is clearly asking for it. Even occasional massage has real benefits.

## A simple rule of thumb

| Your goal | Suggested rhythm |
| --- | --- |
| Pain / chronic tension | Weekly to bi-weekly |
| Athletic recovery | Every 2–4 weeks |
| Stress & maintenance | Monthly |
| Occasional reset | As needed |

The best routine is the one you'll actually keep. Many clients book a standing monthly appointment so self-care doesn't get crowded out of a busy schedule.
